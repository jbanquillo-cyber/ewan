✨ About Me

From the front line to the big picture

My fintech story started at the helpdesk, where I learned exactly how real people use financial products — and what happens when something doesn't work. Curiosity about why issues happened led me into Quality Assurance, where I made sure every transaction, screen, and release was accurate and reliable.

Today, as a Business Analyst, I bring all of that together. I translate business goals into clear requirements, keep developers and stakeholders on the same page, and never lose sight of the end user. I love turning messy problems into simple, well-documented solutions.

Mobile App Prototype · Personal Project

Your wardrobe, planned with care 💗

Dress to Impress helps people organize their clothes, preview outfit combinations on their own photo, check rough measurement fit, and plan what to wear for upcoming events. I approached it the way I approach every product: start from the user's needs, map what each feature depends on, and plan for what can go wrong.

🧭

Clear user flow

A guided 4-step journey: body profile → add clothing → virtual try-on → outfit planner.

🔗

Dependency mapping

Documented each feature's inputs, services, and outputs, plus clear service boundaries.

🧪

Error testing built in

9 failure scenarios, such as network loss or invalid measurements, each with a recovery message.

📏

Honest fit guidance

Rule-based measurement checks that explain uncertainty instead of over-promising results.
